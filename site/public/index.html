Disk analyzer for macOS 12 or later

See where your disk space went. Get it back safely.

ebb reads your whole disk in about a minute, explains every large item and cleans only what you approve. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Nothing runs before you approve it.

Every cleanup starts as a plan. It lists each action, its size, how safe it is and anything blocking it.

It will never

  • Touch anything outside the folder it scanned
  • Touch system folders, Keychains, iCloud or your protect list
  • Remove anything that changed since the plan was made
  • Uninstall an app while it is running
  • Run as root
  • Send anything off your Mac: no account, no telemetry

Your own files go to the Trash, so ebb undo can put them back.

Scan, read, plan, undo.

  1. $ ebb scan

    About a minute for 6 million files the first time. After that ebb follows FSEvents and reads only the folders that changed.

  2. $ ebb findings

    What takes space, why it is there and how safe it is to remove.

  3. $ ebb clean --preset dev-caches

    Shows the plan, then runs it after you confirm.

  4. $ ebb undo

    Puts back what went to the Trash.

  5. every command takes --json

Agents plan. You approve.

ebb assistant install registers ebb's MCP server with your AI tools. An agent can scan, read findings, explain any path and make a cleanup plan. The plan waits until you approve it in the window or with ebb approve.
